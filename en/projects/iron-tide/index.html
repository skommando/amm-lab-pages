Helm & Salvo

HELM & SALVO

Take the helm of a warship. Adjust your course in battle, watch where your shells land and decide when to fire. Helm & Salvo is in development for Windows, with ship handling and gunnery as the current focus.

What the game is built around

01 / HELM & SALVO

Take the helm

Handle your own warship and choose your heading and range. When you turn, consider incoming fire and how much of your hull you expose.

02 / HELM & SALVO

Watch your shots

Watch where your shells land, then correct the next shot. Making that feedback clear is a current focus of development.

03 / HELM & SALVO

Carry your choices forward

Expeditions are being designed around the same flagship, with choices between battles shaping the rest of the voyage. The details are still in development.

Before you follow along

Where can I download it?

The game has not been released. There is no public download or demo at present. A verified store link will appear here when available.

Does it have multiplayer?

The current version is designed for single-player play and does not include multiplayer.

Does it recreate historical ships and battles?

It uses a fictional setting with World War II-era technology. Ship handling and combat decisions take priority over exact historical reconstruction.

When will it release, and what hardware does it need?

The release date, price and final system requirements have not been announced. Hardware requirements will be confirmed after testing the release build on real PCs.
